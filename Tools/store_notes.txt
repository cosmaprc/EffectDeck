Restart your iPhone or iPad once after this update (the device, not the app). iOS reads the name of EffectDeck's audio device only at boot, and this update changes it. Until you restart, EffectDeck may not appear in Control Center, and the main screen says Restart Required. Only this update needs it. A fresh install does not.

New since 2026.10.04.

Output Correction
- Pick a preset for each pair of headphones or speaker. It is applied after the chain, switches by itself when the output changes, and never changes the chain.
- Open it from the Output Correction row below the chain, or from Presets. The row has the on/off switch.

Plug-ins and lists
- Third-party AUv3 plug-ins appear under Plugins. The list updates when you install one or come back to the app.
- Available Effects has three tabs: Effects, Plugins and Presets. Import JSFX is at the top of Plugins, and System Presets are at the end of Presets.

The chain
- Effects can be dropped beside and between cards, below the last card, onto an empty chain, and, on iPad, onto the left list.
- Dropping below the last card works again after Reset chain or deleting a card.

JSFX
- Scripts run about 1.3 to 1.4 times faster on iPhone. The sound is bit for bit the same.
- A script using x[] inside a function that appears more than once could give wrong values or crash. Fixed.
- file_rewind or file_riff on a handle that is not open no longer closes the app.

Previous updates (2026.09.28):

iPad
- A wide window shows the chain as a list on the left and every card open on the right. Reorder in the list, and drop effects into it.

JSFX
EffectDeck hosts JSFX, the single-file script format REAPER uses.
- Import from Files, a link, the clipboard or the share sheet.
- Sliders keep their ranges, curves and menus. Scripts that draw (@gfx) get a canvas with touch, keyboard, menus and full screen.
- @serialize state is saved with the chain and presets. Reported latency is compensated.
- A script that keeps overrunning its time budget is bypassed, and the card says by how much. Re-enable puts it back.
- In Plugins, the ... next to a script offers View Source, Copy Source, Share as a link and Delete.

EffeTune 2.11.0 (DSP 0.11.0)
- New: Attack Tonal Balance, Bass Extender, Chroma Spiral, Bass Management.
- Oversampling up to 8x on Saturation, Dynamic Saturation, Exciter, Harmonic Distortion and Multiband Saturation, 16x on Hard Clipping.

Presets and sharing
- Presets > Import from clipboard reads a chain pasted as text.
- Share this chain makes an effectdeck.nemut.ai link that opens EffectDeck and keeps AU and JSFX stages.
- 15Band PEQ imports an AutoEq ParametricEQ.txt.

Known
- Spotify with Canvas, and sometimes Spotify or YouTube, cannot connect because iOS treats them as video. See Known limitations and issues #1, #3 and #4.
- Overnight battery use is still being measured (issue #5).
